Agent-driven data collection and processing system

A system that collects, deduplicates and ranks web data every 30 minutes, writes tailored documents with an LLM, and hands online form filling to a browser agent, with a safeguard at every stage.

Architecture

The pilot runs the chain continuously on a local machine: collection brings in what is new, triage ranks it, the LLM writes, the browser agent acts, the mailbox reports back, and the dashboard brings it all together.

  1. 1

    Collection

    Ten sources: direct connectors (API, search index, pages and structured data), a scraping library and feeds. Deduplication across sources.

  2. 2

    Triage and writing

    Sourced rules and a priority score, then tailored documents written by an LLM, with facts frozen by code.

  3. 3

    The browser agent

    It fills in forms step by step, with a screenshot and a log at every step.

  4. 4

    Pilot and tracking

    A priority queue, a pause switch, and a mailbox read and sorted automatically.

What can break, and what prevents it

Stage

What can break

What prevents it

  1. Collection

    every 30 minutes

    A source is down or changes.

    That source's batch comes back empty, the others carry on.

  2. Deduplication

    identifier · title

    The same entry published on two sources.

    Merged by identifier, or by title and a shared organisation.

  3. Sorting

    rules · score

    Keeping what does not fit.

    Sourced rules: an exclusion without a source fails a test.

  4. Writing

    LLM

    The LLM makes up a fact.

    Verifiable facts are copied by the code; the LLM writes around them and cannot change them.

  5. Browser agent

    Playwright · Chromium

    Going in circles, forcing a block, acting on a stale entry.

    40 actions at most, a screenshot at every step. A captcha or a limit sets the entry aside; on the logged-in site the agent stops for 20 hours. Entries older than 3 days are dropped.

  6. Pilot

    priority queue

    A silent stall.

    A watchdog restarts after 10 minutes without progress; a pause file stops everything except collection.

  7. Replies

    IMAP

    Misreading or changing the mailbox.

    Read-only. Replies are classified, follow-ups are saved as drafts and sent with one click.

Before each agent restart, 7 real journeys with a known outcome are replayed: two errors that reached production showed that test pages were not enough.

Technical choices

Sorting

Rules over a local classifier

Tested: 2.8 seconds per entry on CPU, and it would have dropped 90% of the entries kept.

Freshness

Real publication date over the date it surfaced

A reposted entry does not pass for a new one.

Reliability

Fixes tested on real cases

Every error seen in production becomes a test, from the anonymised real case.
