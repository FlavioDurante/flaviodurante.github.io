AIDATAPME · Deployed on GCP · first client signed

Andrea, forecasting suite for e‑commerce SMEs

The client estimates sales, Andrea decides stock. Every night, in each client's own GCP project, 8 models turn the shop's, advertising and bank data into decisions read in the morning.

Architecture

Each client has their own GCP project, in the EU region. Every night, GCP Workflows runs the chain: Airbyte pulls the data from the shop's tools, dbt prepares it in BigQuery, the models run on Cloud Run, and the dashboard is up to date in the morning.

  1. 1

    The sources

    Shopify, Meta Ads, Google Ads, Klaviyo and the bank, connected through Airbyte and OAuth.

  2. 2

    Preparation

    dbt on BigQuery: cleaning, pseudonymisation and features computed relative to each shop.

  3. 3

    The models

    Three packs to choose from, on Cloud Run. All their predictions land in one shared store where they cross: the cash simulator tests a stock decision from the stock forecast.

  4. 4

    The dashboard

    It reads the forecasts inside the client's project, without copying them anywhere else.

AIDATAPME's control plane keeps no client data: it reaches each project through identity delegation.

Three questions, three packs

The models do not run each in their own corner. Each segmentation feeds the forecasts of its pack, the demand forecast hands products that are too young to the new-products model, and the cash forecast reads the return probability of each order. All predictions cross in one shared store.

Stock

How much to order?

The recommended order for each product, at the stock-out risk the shop accepts. For a new product, its look-alike products and its launch curve.

  • Catalogue segmentation HDBSCAN + LightGBM

    Star, standard and dead-weight products.

  • Demand forecast LightGBM

    Daily quantities per product, with lags from 7 to 365 days and Fourier seasonalities.

  • New products k-NN, content embedding, Product2Vec

    Products with less than 45 days of history, handed over by the demand forecast: it starts from look-alike products, shown to the client.

On a large catalogue. For 75,000 products across four seasons: a two-part model for intermittent demand, category → family → product reconciliation, UMAP + HDBSCAN season by season, and conformal prediction for the interval.

Retention

Who is about to leave?

VIP, loyal and dormant customers, and the ones to win back before they drop off.

  • Customer segmentation HDBSCAN + LightGBM

    VIP, loyal and dormant.

  • Churn risk LightGBM, point-in-time join

    A churn score per customer, reading their segment as it was at each date.

  • Returns, optional LightGBM

    The return probability of each order, read by the cash forecast.

The trap avoided. The point-in-time join: at each training date, the model only sees the segment known on that day. No information comes from the future.

Cash

When does cash get tight?

The curve of the next 12 weeks, from bank flows (Powens), with a simulator to test a stock decision.

  • Cost segmentation HDBSCAN

    Fixed and volatile costs.

  • 12-week cash Prophet with regressors

    The cash curve for the next three months.

The source. Bank flows come in through Powens. The forecast reads the split between fixed and volatile costs made just before it.

Technical choices

Data isolation

One GCP project per client over a shared project

Raw data, pseudonymised data and forecasts stay in the client's project. The control plane keeps none of it and reaches it through identity delegation.

Pseudonymisation

SHA-256 with a per-client salt over HMAC

The same protection against re-identification for this need, with no function to maintain. Each client has their own salt: a leak affects one client only.

Business rules

Relative thresholds over fixed thresholds

Any shop-specific threshold is computed on the shop's own data, for example above its 95th percentile. One dbt codebase serves every shop.

Preparation

dbt decoupled from the dashboard

dbt runs as a scheduled job and writes to BigQuery; the dashboard only reads. A failure on one side does not stop the other.

How the project ran

  1. 01

    The diagnostic

    An audit tool on a shop's Shopify export: stock-outs of star products, dormant customers, products returned too often. It shows an SME what its own data says.

  2. 02

    The models

    The 8 models in Python, tested on the real history of an online shop.

  3. 03

    The pipeline

    dbt on BigQuery: preparation views for each source, then one feature table per product and per day. Nightly orchestration with GCP Workflows, infrastructure in Terraform.

  4. 04

    Many clients, GDPR included

    One isolated GCP project per client, pseudonymisation with a per-client salt, the EU region. The same code serves every shop.

  5. 05

    The dashboard

    React and TypeScript: the Stock, Retention and Cash modules, each with its real figures.

  6. 06

    An architecture for 75,000 products

    For a four-season catalogue of components: two-part model for intermittent demand, category → family → product reconciliation, UMAP + HDBSCAN season by season, SHAP and conformal prediction. Architecture designed and priced, with a validation protocol to be judged by the client's team against their current system.

  7. 07

    The first client

    Contract and data processing agreement signed as a pilot. The dbt pipeline and pseudonymisation are deployed on GCP; the sources are connected together with the client.
